HubSpot vs Zoho CRM for a Small Business

Both vendors position their CRM as free beyond a trial. HubSpot emphasizes a unified customer platform and no expiration, while Zoho says its free CRM supports up to three users and core records such as leads, contacts, accounts, deals, tasks, and events. Verify current limits before choosing.

Decision areaHubSpotZoho CRM
Free positioningVendor says 100% free with no expiration and no card required.Vendor says free forever, no card required, for up to three users.
Best initial fitA business considering a broad marketing, sales, service, and content platform.A very small team wanting structured CRM records and the wider Zoho application ecosystem.
Workflow testTest contact, deal, task, form, and email workflows you actually need.Test lead conversion, contacts, accounts, deals, tasks, events, and exports.
Growth questionWhich premium hubs, seats, and limits become necessary?Which edition is needed for more users, records, customization, or automation?

Fast decision rule

Start with HubSpot when the broad customer-platform path matters more than a compact CRM-only setup. Start with Zoho CRM when a team of up to three wants structured sales records and may use adjacent Zoho apps. Choose neither if a spreadsheet still handles every opportunity reliably.

Run the same test in both

  1. Create five sample contacts and two opportunities.
  2. Assign a next action and due date.
  3. Capture one test lead through a form.
  4. Log an email or note.
  5. Export all test records.
  6. Price the likely paid configuration at twice today's team and contact volume.
