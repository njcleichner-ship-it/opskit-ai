FAQ

What format is the kit?

The kit is delivered as copyable template files, including Markdown and printable HTML versions.

Do I need special software?

No. You can copy the templates into Google Docs, Notion, your CRM, a form builder, or your notes app.

Does this replace a CRM?

No. It is a lightweight workflow and template pack for intake and follow-up. It can work alongside a CRM, but it is not a full CRM.

Will this guarantee more clients?

No. It helps you run a cleaner process, ask better questions, and follow up faster. It does not guarantee sales.

Can I share it with my team?

One purchase covers one individual or one business for internal use. You cannot resell or redistribute the kit as a standalone product.
