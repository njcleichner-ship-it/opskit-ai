Free Email Marketing Tools for Small Lists

Early-stage newsletters and customer lists usually need simple sending, signup forms, clean exports, and clear upgrade pricing.

Compare these limits

  • Subscriber/contact cap
  • Monthly send cap
  • Automation access
  • Landing pages and forms
  • Required branding
  • Compliance tools such as unsubscribe handling

Free-first tools to evaluate include MailerLite, Brevo, Substack, Buttondown, ConvertKit alternatives, and Beehiiv alternatives. Verify current pricing before choosing.
