Free Newsletter Platforms to Evaluate

A newsletter platform should help you publish consistently, collect subscribers with consent, export your list, and understand what readers engage with. Free tiers are useful for testing fit before committing to monthly software.

Use caseWhat to comparePlatforms to evaluate
Simple personal newsletterFast editor, signup page, clean exportsSubstack, Buttondown, beehiiv alternatives
Creator businessLanding pages, recommendations, sponsorship optionsbeehiiv, Kit, Substack alternatives
Small business listForms, basic campaigns, segmentation, integrationsBrevo, MailerLite, Kit alternatives
Publisher-style growthReferral tools, analytics, ad/sponsor workflowsbeehiiv alternatives and publisher-focused platforms

Free-first rule

Start with the platform that gets one useful issue published every week. Upgrade only when the list is growing, revenue is clear, or the free plan blocks a real workflow.
