# Monetization Readiness Checklist — CalendarCalc Pages

## Before applying for ads
- [ ] Public site is live and crawlable.
- [ ] Site has useful original tools/content, not thin placeholder pages.
- [ ] Privacy page is live.
- [ ] Clear navigation exists.
- [ ] No copyrighted content theft.
- [ ] No fake traffic, click prompts, ad manipulation, or misleading labels.
- [ ] Add contact/about page if required by ad network.
- [ ] Add more original utility pages before AdSense application.

## Likely owner action later
AdSense or other ad network approval will likely require an owner-controlled account, site ownership verification, tax/payment profile, and policy acceptance. Do not ask until the site has enough useful content to be review-ready.

- Deadline calculator pages for project managers and freelancers.
- Time zone meeting helper.
