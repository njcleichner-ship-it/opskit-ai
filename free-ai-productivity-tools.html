Free AI Productivity Tools for Solo Operators

AI tools can help draft outlines, summarize notes, generate checklists, compare options, and speed up routine writing. Free tiers are useful for testing fit before paying.

Good use cases

  • Drafting first-pass emails and outlines
  • Summarizing meeting notes
  • Brainstorming content angles
  • Turning rough notes into checklists
  • Research assistance with source checking

Tools to evaluate

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Notion AI alternatives, and document-specific AI assistants. Do not paste sensitive customer or payment data into tools without understanding privacy terms.
