HubSpot Alternatives for Small Businesses

HubSpot publicly offers a free CRM with no expiration, making it a reasonable baseline. An alternative may fit better when you need a simpler pipeline, a small-team free allowance, tighter email integration, or a lower-cost upgrade path.

Alternatives to evaluate

  • Zoho CRM: its public free-plan page says up to three users can manage leads, contacts, deals, tasks, and events.
  • Brevo Sales: its public page offers a custom sales pipeline on the free plan and connects sales management with marketing capabilities.
  • Bigin by Zoho CRM: positioned by Zoho as a simpler, pipeline-centric option for small businesses; verify current pricing before choosing.
  • A spreadsheet plus forms: often enough before a solo business has a repeatable sales process.

Decision rule

Do not switch for a longer feature list. Switch when the current tool creates a measurable problem in data ownership, workflow speed, collaboration, integrations, or total cost.
