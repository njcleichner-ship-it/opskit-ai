Free CRM Options for Consultants

A consultant usually needs fewer CRM features than a sales department. The system must capture inquiries, show the next follow-up, track proposals, and hand won work into delivery without losing context.

OptionEvaluate whenVerify first
HubSpot CRMYou want a broad free CRM baseline and may later connect marketing, sales, or service tools.Current free limits and the paid configuration required for automation or reporting.
Zoho CRMUp to three users need structured leads, contacts, accounts, deals, tasks, and events.Record allowance, integrations, exports, and the edition required after growth.
BiginA solo consultant wants one simple pipeline and a smaller setup surface.The reviewed free edition was single-user with 500 records; confirm current limits.
Spreadsheet plus formOnly a few opportunities are active and reminders are still reliable.Access control, backups, duplicate handling, and a clear trigger for moving to a CRM.

A useful consulting pipeline

  1. New inquiry
  2. Qualified
  3. Discovery scheduled
  4. Proposal sent
  5. Decision pending
  6. Won / handoff
  7. Lost / nurture with consent

Minimum viable fields

Contact, company, service requested, source, expected value, current stage, next action, next-action date, proposal link, and consent status where relevant. Avoid collecting sensitive client information in a sales CRM unless there is a clear need and appropriate protection.

Test before importing everything

  • Capture a test inquiry from a form.
  • Schedule a discovery follow-up.
  • Move a sample deal through every stage.
  • Attach or link a proposal.
  • Mark a deal won and verify the delivery handoff.
  • Export the complete record and activity history.
