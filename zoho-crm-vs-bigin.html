Zoho CRM vs Bigin for a Small Business

Zoho positions Bigin as a simpler, pipeline-centric CRM for small businesses graduating from spreadsheets. Zoho CRM is the broader flagship system. The practical choice is whether simplicity or deeper CRM structure is the immediate priority.

AreaZoho CRM free editionBigin free edition
Published free allowanceUp to three users.Single user.
Published record allowanceUp to 5,000 records across modules on the reviewed free page.500 records across pipelines, contacts, activities, and notes on the reviewed pricing page.
Operating modelLeads, contacts, accounts, deals, tasks, events, reports, and broader CRM structure.One visual pipeline with contacts, products, activities, forms, and a smaller setup surface.
Likely fitA small team that needs lead conversion, account relationships, or more structured sales records.A solo operator who wants a straightforward customer pipeline without adopting a larger CRM.

Choose Bigin when

One person needs a simple pipeline, 500 records are enough for the starting workflow, and a larger CRM would create avoidable setup work.

Choose Zoho CRM when

Up to three users need the system, lead/account/deal structure matters, or the expected record count and workflow require the broader CRM model.

Questions before migration

  • Does each row in the current spreadsheet represent a person, company, or opportunity?
  • Do you need separate lead qualification before creating contacts and deals?
  • Will one pipeline represent the entire customer process?
  • Which email, forms, invoicing, or support integrations are required at the chosen edition?
  • Can the test records and activity history be exported cleanly?
