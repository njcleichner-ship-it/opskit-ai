Kit Alternatives for Creators and Newsletters

Kit, formerly ConvertKit, is built around creator email marketing. But the best alternative depends on whether you need a simple newsletter, audience growth tools, paid products, automation, or small-business campaigns.

NeedLook forAlternatives to evaluate
Simple newsletterFast writing workflow, signup page, exportsSubstack, Buttondown, beehiiv alternatives
Creator growthRecommendations, landing pages, audience analyticsbeehiiv, Substack alternatives
Small-business emailCampaigns, forms, segmentation, integrationsBrevo, MailerLite, Mailchimp alternatives
Paid productsCommerce features, delivery, low frictionKit, Gumroad-style tools, newsletter platforms with paid options

Before switching, compare current free limits, automation access, deliverability controls, export options, and the total cost once the list grows.
