Solo-Founder CRM Starter Stack

A solo founder does not need enterprise sales software. The minimum viable system is one capture point, one contact record, one pipeline, one follow-up routine, and a reliable export.

The stack

  1. Lead form: collect a name, reply address, need, and consent.
  2. CRM: use a free CRM or spreadsheet with stages such as New, Qualified, Proposal, Won, Lost.
  3. Calendar: offer a booking link only when a call is the right next step.
  4. Templates: keep a few honest follow-up drafts, then personalize each response.
  5. Weekly review: close stale records, schedule next actions, and export a backup.

Upgrade trigger

Pay for automation only when manual follow-up is causing missed opportunities or consuming more value than the subscription costs.

Evaluate free CRM options, HubSpot alternatives, and lead-capture tools.

Use permission-based outreach only; do not upload scraped or purchased lists.
