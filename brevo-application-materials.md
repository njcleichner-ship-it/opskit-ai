## Short site description
FreeStack Finder is a free software decision-helper site for solo founders, freelancers, creators, and small businesses. It helps visitors compare free tiers, free trials, and low-cost business tools for email marketing, scheduling, forms, invoicing, AI productivity, and automation.

## Why Brevo fits
Brevo is relevant to the email marketing category because early-stage operators often need a free-first email, CRM, and marketing automation tool before they are ready for paid software. The current site already has a free email marketing tools page and can expand into comparison and migration guides.

## Traffic plan
- Build original SEO pages around free email marketing tools, free CRM/email combinations, newsletter starter stacks, and small-business marketing software comparisons.
- Avoid spam, fake traffic, paid click manipulation, fake reviews, or misleading rankings.
- Use clear affiliate disclosures and keep non-affiliate alternatives where useful.
- Add affiliate links only after acceptance and only in relevant pages.

## Compliance posture
- No fake product reviews or claims of hands-on use unless actually tested.
- No undisclosed sponsored placements.
- No scraping protected content or copying vendor copy wholesale.
- No incentivized clicks or click prompts.
- Recommendations should explain fit, limits to verify, and who should consider each tool.
