Brevo Alternatives for Free-First Email Marketing

Brevo is worth evaluating for small-business email marketing, but it is not the only option. The right alternative depends on whether you need a newsletter, CRM-style contacts, landing pages, automation, or simple publishing.

Alternative categories

  • Newsletter-first: Substack, Buttondown, Beehiiv alternatives
  • Marketing automation: MailerLite, ConvertKit alternatives, Mailchimp alternatives
  • CRM plus email: HubSpot alternatives and lightweight CRM tools
  • Creator publishing: platforms focused on audience growth and recurring publishing

When Brevo may fit

Brevo may be a good fit when a small business wants email marketing plus broader customer communication features. Always verify current pricing, limits, and terms directly with the vendor.
