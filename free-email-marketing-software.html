Best Free Email Marketing Software to Evaluate

For a new list, free email marketing software should make it easy to collect subscribers, send useful updates, and leave cleanly if the tool stops fitting. Do not choose only by the largest free limit; choose by the workflow you will actually use.

Use caseWhat to prioritizeTools to evaluate
Simple newsletterEasy editor, signup page, clean exportsSubstack, Buttondown, Beehiiv alternatives
Small business marketingForms, campaigns, basic automation, contact managementBrevo, MailerLite, ConvertKit alternatives
Creator audienceLanding pages, recommendations, publishing workflowBeehiiv alternatives, Substack, ConvertKit alternatives
Transactional or CRM-adjacent emailContacts, segmentation, integrations, deliverability controlsBrevo, HubSpot alternatives, Mailchimp alternatives

Fast selection rule

If you have under 100 subscribers, pick the tool that lets you publish consistently and export your list. Upgrade only when automation, segmentation, or revenue justifies the cost.
