Free Lead-Capture Tools and Workflow

Lead capture should make it easy for an interested person to ask for help while keeping consent, context, and the next action clear. Never buy, scrape, or import people who did not ask to hear from you.

StepFree-first approachWhat matters
CaptureGoogle Forms, Tally, CRM web formsShort form, clear purpose, consent language
StoreHubSpot, Zoho CRM, Brevo Sales, spreadsheetExport access, deduplication, owner and status fields
QualifyRequired problem, budget, timing, or use-case fieldsAsk only what changes the next action
RespondEmail template plus scheduling linkFast, relevant, non-automated-looking follow-up

Minimum fields

Name, email, what they need, consent to receive a response, lead source, status, and next action are enough for many solo businesses.
