Free Scheduling Tools for Small Businesses

Free scheduling tools are useful when you need a booking link for sales calls, consultations, interviews, office hours, or onboarding calls.

Features to compare

  • Number of active booking links
  • Calendar sync support
  • Buffer times between meetings
  • Email reminders
  • Payment collection if needed later
  • Branding and custom questions

Free-first shortlist to evaluate

Calendly, Cal.com, Google Calendar appointment schedules, and other booking tools with usable free tiers. Verify current limits before choosing.
