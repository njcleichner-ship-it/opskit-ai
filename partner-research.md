# FreeStack Finder Partner Research

## Monetization target
Compliant affiliate/partner links for software where a visitor can start with a free trial, free tier, or low-cost signup. No fake reviews, fake traffic, click prompts, or undisclosed sponsored rankings.

## Initial research results — 2026-08-06

### Calendly
- Checked: https://calendly.com/partners
- Result: Calendly has partner/API positioning and invites partner conversations, but the fetched page did not show a simple public affiliate payout program.
- Fit: Useful to list as a non-affiliate/free-first scheduling option; partner monetization may require direct application/conversation later.

### Brevo
- Checked: https://www.brevo.com/partners/
- Result: Brevo publicly describes an affiliate program for influencers, bloggers, and publishers that rewards referrals.
- Fit: Strong early candidate for the email marketing category once the site has enough content for application.

### Jotform

- Result: URL returned 404 in fetch.
- Fit: Keep as useful non-affiliate form-builder mention for now; research alternate partner URL later.

### MailerLite

- Result: URL returned 404, but the site footer includes “Become a partner”.
- Fit: Research partner route later; do not claim affiliate availability until verified.

### Make

- Fit: Needs alternate source or manual browser verification later; do not claim availability yet.

## Priority candidates
1. Brevo affiliate program — best verified fit from initial fetch.
2. Other email marketing and automation tools with public partner pages.
3. Scheduling/forms tools as useful content even if not immediately monetized.

## Acceptance requirements to check before applying
- Does the program allow content/SEO sites?
- Does it pay for free trials, free signups, paid upgrades, or only purchases?
- Are disclosure requirements clear?
- Any prohibited traffic sources?
- Does signup require owner identity, tax, or payment account action?
