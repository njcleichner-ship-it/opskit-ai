Free Invoicing and Payment Tools

For a small business, the best payment tool is usually the one that lets customers pay easily and makes records simple. Free setup does not mean zero processing fees.

Compare before choosing

  • Payment processing fees
  • Invoice templates
  • Recurring invoice support
  • Tax settings and exports
  • Payment links
  • Customer experience

Tools to evaluate include Stripe, PayPal, Square, Wave, and accounting-platform invoice tools.
