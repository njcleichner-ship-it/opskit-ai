Newsletter Starter Stack for Solo Founders

A newsletter stack does not need to be expensive. A solo founder can start with a free email platform, one signup page, a simple publishing routine, and a spreadsheet or notes app for ideas.

Minimum viable stack

  • Email platform with signup forms and unsubscribe handling
  • One landing page that explains who the newsletter helps
  • Simple content calendar with 4 to 8 planned issues
  • Analytics limited to opens, clicks, replies, and subscriber growth
  • Export habit so the list is not trapped in one tool

What to avoid

Do not buy automation before you have a clear audience. Do not import people without permission. Do not use scraped email lists. Legitimate email marketing starts with consent.

Tools to evaluate include Brevo, MailerLite, Substack, Buttondown, ConvertKit alternatives, and Beehiiv alternatives. Verify current free limits before committing.
